The Lalela Community source code is open source under the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Our names and logos are not. This policy explains
what you may and may not do with them. It is modelled on the trademark policies
of Mozilla and the Rust project, written in plain English.

registered:

- the name **Lalela**, and any name that could be confused with it;
- the Lalela logo, wordmark and **app icons** (everything under

- the names **Auxo** and **Auxo Digital** and their logos;
- the name **Amp** as used for our design system, and the **Spark** mascot.

The Apache License gives you rights to the code. Section 6 of that licence
says it does not give you rights to our trademarks. This policy is the only
permission you have to use them.

## What you can do without asking

- **Build and use it yourself.** You may build Lalela Community from this
  repository, unmodified, and run it on your own computers, including at work.
  The build keeps the name "Lalela Community" and our icons; that is fine.
- **Talk about it.** You may use the name Lalela in plain text to refer to the
  project truthfully: in articles, reviews, tutorials, talks, package listings
  that point to this repository, and so on.
- **Say what your work is based on.** If you ship a modified version under your
  own name, you may say it is "based on Lalela Community" or "a fork of Lalela"
  in its description, as long as the description is accurate and your product's
  own name and icon are clearly different.
- **Contribute.** Pull requests to this repository can of course mention and
  modify the project's own branding files.

## What needs a different name and icon

If you **distribute a modified version** of the app (a fork, a repackaged
build, a version with features added or removed, or a build with a different
configuration that changes what it connects to), you must:

1. give it a name that is not "Lalela" and not confusingly similar to it;
2. replace the app icons and logos with your own;
3. change the bundle identifier (`identifier` in
   `apps/desktop/src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`) so it cannot be mistaken for, or
   overwrite, our apps;
4. not suggest that we made, endorse or support your version.

Distributing an **unmodified** build to others (for example, inside your
company) is allowed if you keep the "Lalela Community" name, do not charge for
the build itself, and make clear it is the community edition built from this
repository, not the official app.

## What you can never do

- Use our marks for an app, service or website that is not this project, or in
  a way that suggests it comes from us.
- Publish any build to an app store, package registry or download site under
  the Lalela name or with our icons, except unmodified packages that point
  users back to this repository.
- Use our marks in a domain name, social media handle or company name.
- Use our marks in a way that is misleading, disparaging or illegal.

## Asking for permission

If you want to do something this policy does not allow, or you are not sure,
email **hello@auxo.digital** before you do it. We are happy to help
community projects use the name in good faith.

We may update this policy. The version in this repository's default branch
is the current one.
